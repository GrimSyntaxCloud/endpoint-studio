02 / UNDER THE SURFACE

Real endpoints.
Room to explore.

Recovered from Andres’s REST API coursework. A new interface makes the original composers, people, teams, customer invoices, and signup/login exercises tangible.

01

Your own small world.

Each private workspace has an unguessable access token. Other visitors cannot browse your records. Use fictional details and a throwaway demo password.

02

Built to forget.

Visitor workspaces expire 96 hours after creation. Edits do not extend the deadline. Permanent fictional samples remain available.

03

A clear boundary.

Signup and login demonstrate password verification inside your workspace; they are not a production identity service. This is a learning demo.
